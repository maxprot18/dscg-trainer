# Reading the independent auditor’s report

**References:** ISA 700 (Revised), 570 (Revised), 701, 705 (Revised), 706 (Revised) and 720 (Revised) (IAASB) ; PCAOB AS 3101 (critical audit matters) ; Regulation (EU) No 537/2014, art. 10 ; NEP 700, 701 and 702

**Key issue:** the auditor's report is the only part of the annual report written by an independent party; the exam gives an extract and asks what the opinion is, whether a given section modifies it, and what the auditor does or does not cover.

**Typical structure of a report on a listed company (ISA 700):**
- **Opinion** first: identifies the entity, the statements audited and the framework, then states that the financial statements "give a true and fair view" (UK/EU wording) or "present fairly, in all material respects" (fair presentation wording, used in US reports). A modified opinion is headed "Qualified opinion", "Adverse opinion" or "Disclaimer of opinion".
- **Basis for opinion**: audit conducted under ISAs, independence and ethical requirements, sufficient appropriate audit evidence obtained. When the opinion is modified, this section becomes "Basis for qualified (adverse) opinion" and describes the matter and its amount.
- **Material uncertainty related to going concern** (ISA 570), when relevant: a separate section that refers to the note where the directors describe the uncertainty; the opinion is not modified if the disclosure is adequate. For periods beginning on or after 15 December 2026, ISA 570 (Revised 2024) requires a separate section even when no material uncertainty exists (heading "Going concern"), with explicit conclusions on whether the going concern basis is appropriate and whether a material uncertainty has been identified.
- **Key audit matters** (ISA 701): the matters of most significance in the audit (e.g. goodwill impairment, revenue recognition, provisions, IT systems), why each was significant and how it was addressed. The section gives no separate opinion on these matters. US equivalent: **critical audit matters** (CAMs, AS 3101).
- **Other information** (ISA 720): the auditor reads the rest of the annual report for material inconsistencies with the financial statements or with the knowledge obtained during the audit, but gives no opinion on it.
- **Responsibilities** of management and those charged with governance (preparing the accounts, assessing going concern) and of the auditor (reasonable assurance, professional judgement and scepticism, identification of risks, evaluation of estimates, group audit).
- **Report on other legal and regulatory requirements**, the name of the engagement partner, signature, address and date (after the directors' approval of the accounts).

**EU public-interest entities** (Regulation 537/2014, art. 10): the report also states the date of appointment and the total uninterrupted engagement period, confirms that no prohibited non-audit services were provided and that the opinion is consistent with the additional report to the audit committee.

**France**: *rapport sur les comptes annuels / consolidés* ; for PIEs, KAM appear in the *justification des appréciations – points clés de l'audit* (NEP 701; other entities: NEP 702, no KAM) ; *observation* = emphasis of matter ; the *vérifications spécifiques* section (NEP 9510, revised 2026) plays the role of the other information section.

## Example
Extract: "Material uncertainty related to going concern. We draw attention to note 2, which indicates that the renewal of the group's €150m revolving credit facility, due in six months, has not yet been agreed. These events indicate that a material uncertainty exists... Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter." The Opinion section states that the accounts give a true and fair view.
- Solution: unmodified opinion; the auditor considers the going concern basis appropriate and the disclosure in note 2 adequate, and simply draws users' attention to it.
- Had the directors refused to disclose the uncertainty, the auditor would have qualified the opinion (or expressed an adverse opinion if the omission was pervasive); had the going concern basis been inappropriate, the opinion would have been adverse.

## Common mistakes
- Reading the going concern section as a qualification: it does not modify the opinion when the disclosure is adequate; the sentence "our opinion is not modified" is explicit.
- Expecting a list of all misstatements found, or fees per non-audit service, in an EU report: article 10 requires the appointment date and engagement period, a statement on prohibited non-audit services and consistency with the additional report, not those details.
- Believing that KAM express an opinion on each matter or that the strategic report and MD&A are audited: KAM describe the audit work, and the front half is other information.
- Expecting an opinion on internal control over financial reporting in every report: this exists in US reports on accelerated and large accelerated filers (SOX s.404(b)), not under ISA 700.

## Key points
- Order of sections: Opinion, Basis for opinion, Going concern (if any), KAM, Other information, Responsibilities, Other requirements.
- KAM and going concern sections do not modify the opinion; only a qualified, adverse or disclaimer heading does.
- "Present fairly" and "true and fair view" express the same idea of fair presentation.
