# Reading the independent auditor's report

**References:** ISA 700 (Revised), 570 (Revised), 701, 705 (Revised), 706 (Revised) and 720 (Revised) (IAASB) ; PCAOB AS 3101 (critical audit matters) ; Regulation (EU) No 537/2014, art. 10 ; NEP 700 and 701

**Typical structure of a report on a listed company (ISA 700):**
- **Opinion** first: the financial statements "give a true and fair view" (UK/EU wording) or "present fairly, in all material respects" (fair presentation wording, used in US reports).
- **Basis for opinion**: audit conducted under ISAs, independence and ethical requirements, sufficient appropriate audit evidence.
- **Material uncertainty related to going concern** (ISA 570), when relevant: a separate section, the opinion is not modified if the disclosure is adequate.
- **Key audit matters** (ISA 701): the matters of most significance in the audit (e.g. goodwill impairment, revenue recognition, provisions), why they were significant and how they were addressed. US equivalent: **critical audit matters** (CAMs).
- **Other information** (ISA 720): the auditor reads the rest of the annual report for material inconsistencies with the financial statements but gives no opinion on it.
- **Responsibilities** of management / those charged with governance (preparing the accounts, going concern assessment) and of the auditor (reasonable assurance, professional scepticism).
- **Other legal and regulatory requirements**, the name of the engagement partner, signature, date.

**EU public-interest entities** (Regulation 537/2014, art. 10): the report also states the date of appointment and the total uninterrupted engagement period, confirms that no prohibited non-audit services were provided and that the opinion is consistent with the additional report to the audit committee.

**France**: *rapport sur les comptes annuels / consolidés* ; KAM appear in the *justification des appréciations – points clés de l'audit* ; *observation* = emphasis of matter.

## Key points
- KAM and going concern sections do not modify the opinion.
- The auditor does not audit the strategic report or the MD&A: it reads them as other information (UK law adds a consistency statement on the strategic and directors' reports).
- "Present fairly" and "true and fair view" express the same idea of fair presentation.
