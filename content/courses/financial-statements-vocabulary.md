# Financial statements vocabulary: balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement

**References:** IAS 1 §10, §54, §66-76, §82 and §99-105 ; IAS 7 §6 and §10-17 ; IFRS 16 §50 (règlement UE 2023/1803)

**Complete set of financial statements (IAS 1 §10):** statement of financial position (balance sheet, *bilan*), statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income (income statement, *compte de résultat*), statement of changes in equity, statement of cash flows (*tableau des flux de trésorerie*) and notes (*annexe*). IFRS 18, issued by the IASB in 2024, is due to replace IAS 1 from 2027 (subject to EU endorsement).

**Balance sheet:** assets (*actif*) = equity (*capitaux propres*) + liabilities (*dettes et provisions*).
- Current vs non-current (IAS 1 §66-76): an asset is current if it is expected to be realised within the normal operating cycle or within twelve months; same logic for liabilities.
- Typical items: property, plant and equipment (PP&E), intangible assets, inventories, trade receivables, cash and cash equivalents; share capital, share premium, retained earnings; borrowings, trade payables, accruals, provisions.

**Income statement:** revenue (*chiffre d'affaires*), cost of sales, gross profit (*marge brute*), distribution costs, administrative expenses, operating profit, finance costs, profit before tax, income tax expense, profit for the year (*résultat net*). Expenses are analysed by nature or by function (IAS 1 §99).

**Cash flow statement (IAS 7 §10):** operating activities (*exploitation*), investing activities (*investissement* : purchase or disposal of PP&E, acquisitions) and financing activities (*financement* : share issues, borrowings, repayments, principal portion of lease payments, IFRS 16 §50).

**Key formulas:** gross profit = revenue − cost of sales ; EBITDA = operating profit + depreciation + amortisation ; current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities ; quick ratio = (current assets − inventories) ÷ current liabilities.

## Key points
- EBITDA is not defined by IFRS: it is an alternative performance measure, to be reconciled with an IFRS line item.
- "Profit" or "income" translates *résultat*; never "result" in a set of accounts.
- Dividends paid may be shown in operating or financing activities (IAS 7 §34), never in investing activities.
- Accruals (*charges à payer*) are liabilities; prepayments (*charges constatées d'avance*) are assets.
