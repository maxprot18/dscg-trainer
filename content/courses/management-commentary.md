# Chairman's statement and management commentary

**References:** IFRS Practice Statement 1 Management Commentary (IASB, non-mandatory) ; SEC Regulation S-K, Item 303 (MD&A) ; ESMA Guidelines on Alternative Performance Measures (ESMA/2015/1415) ; IFRS 8 Operating Segments (règlement UE 2023/1803)

- **Chairman's statement** (letter to shareholders): a short, upbeat overview of the year, the dividend, the board and the outlook. **Chief executive's review**: strategy and operational performance. **Financial review** (CFO): revenue, margins, cash flow, net debt, capital allocation.
- **Management commentary / MD&A**: explains the figures "through the eyes of management": results of operations, liquidity and capital resources, known trends and uncertainties, outlook.
- **Alternative performance measures (APMs)**: measures not defined by IFRS (adjusted EBITDA, underlying profit, organic growth, free cash flow, net debt). ESMA guidelines: define each APM, give its **reconciliation** to the most directly reconcilable line item of the financial statements, explain why it is relevant, present comparatives, use it consistently over time, and do not display it with more prominence than IFRS measures.
- **Organic (like-for-like) growth**: growth excluding the effects of acquisitions, disposals and exchange rates (**constant currency**).
- **Segment reporting (IFRS 8)**: operating segments are those reviewed by the **chief operating decision maker** (CODM); a segment is reportable if its revenue, profit or loss, or assets reach 10% of the combined total; reportable segments must cover at least 75% of external revenue.
- **Vocabulary**: headwinds (unfavourable factors) vs tailwinds, year-on-year (YoY), top line (revenue) vs bottom line (net profit), one-off or exceptional items, guidance (targets announced to the market), run-rate, margin expansion/erosion, basis points (bp: 1 bp = 0.01 percentage point).
- **Forward-looking statements**: comments on future performance, with a cautionary statement that actual results may differ.

**Key formula:** organic growth = (current revenue − acquisitions' contribution ± FX effect) ÷ (prior-year revenue − revenue of disposed businesses) − 1

## Key points
- "Adjusted" or "underlying" figures are management's choices: always look for the reconciliation.
- Organic growth removes disposals from the prior-year base and acquisitions from the current year.
- An improvement of 50 bp means +0.5 percentage point, not +50%.
