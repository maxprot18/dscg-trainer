# Structure of an annual report

**References:** UK Companies Act 2006, s.414A-414D (strategic report) and s.415 (directors' report) ; SEC Form 10-K ; Directive 2013/34/EU ; Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/815 (ESEF) ; Code de commerce, art. L. 232-1 (rapport de gestion)

**UK annual report (listed company)**, in three blocks:
- **Strategic report**: chair's statement, chief executive's review, business model, strategy, key performance indicators (KPIs), **principal risks and uncertainties**, section 172 statement (how directors considered stakeholders), sustainability information.
- **Governance**: board of directors, corporate governance report, committee reports (audit, nomination), **directors' remuneration report**, directors' report.
- **Financial statements**: independent auditor's report, consolidated income statement, statement of comprehensive income, balance sheet, statement of changes in equity, cash flow statement, notes, then the parent company's own accounts.

**US Form 10-K** (filed with the SEC): Item 1 Business, Item 1A Risk Factors, Item 7 Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A), Item 8 Financial Statements and Supplementary Data.

**France**: the *rapport financier annuel* (often included in a *document d'enregistrement universel*, universal registration document), with the *rapport de gestion* (management report), the *rapport sur le gouvernement d'entreprise* and, under the CSRD, the sustainability statement (*état de durabilité*).

**ESEF**: EU issuers prepare their annual financial report in XHTML, with IFRS consolidated statements tagged in iXBRL (machine-readable).

**Vocabulary**: group (consolidated) vs company (parent-only) accounts, financial year (*exercice*), year ended 31 December, comparatives (*chiffres comparatifs*), restated (*retraité*), notes to the financial statements (*annexe*).

## Key points
- The audited part is the financial statements; the narrative front section is "other information" that the auditor reads but does not audit (UK auditors also state whether the strategic report and directors' report are consistent with the financial statements).
- Profit belongs to the income statement; dividends go through the statement of changes in equity.
- In a 10-K, the MD&A is Item 7 and the risk factors are Item 1A.
