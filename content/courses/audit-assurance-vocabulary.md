# Audit and assurance vocabulary

**References:** ISA 200, 320, 450, 501, 505, 570, 580, 701, 705 and 706 (IAASB) ; ISRE 2410 ; NEP, the French standards based on the ISAs

**Players and engagements**
- Statutory auditor (*commissaire aux comptes*), external vs internal auditor, audit committee (*comité d'audit*), engagement letter (*lettre de mission*).
- Audit: reasonable assurance (*assurance raisonnable*) that the financial statements are free from material misstatement and give a true and fair view (*image fidèle*). Review: limited assurance (*assurance modérée/limitée*), e.g. interim financial statements under ISRE 2410.

**Planning and evidence**
- Materiality (*seuil de signification*, ISA 320), often a percentage of a benchmark such as profit before tax; performance materiality (*seuil de planification*) is set lower; the "clearly trivial" threshold (*seuil des anomalies manifestement insignifiantes*).
- Misstatement (*anomalie*), assertion (*assertion*: occurrence, completeness, accuracy, cut-off, existence, valuation…), audit evidence (*éléments probants*), sampling, walkthrough, test of controls.
- External confirmation or circularisation (*circularisation*, ISA 505), stocktake attendance (*assistance à l'inventaire physique*, ISA 501), management representation letter (*lettre d'affirmation*, ISA 580).
- Misstatements other than clearly trivial are accumulated and communicated to management, who is asked to correct them (ISA 450).

**Auditor's report (ISA 705-706)**
- Unmodified opinion (*certification sans réserve*).
- Modified opinions (ISA 705; French terms from NEP 700): qualified (*certification avec réserve*: material, not pervasive), adverse (*refus de certifier*: material and pervasive misstatements), disclaimer of opinion (*impossibilité de certifier*: unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence, possible effects material and pervasive).
- Emphasis of matter paragraph (*observation*), key audit matters (KAM, ISA 701; in French reports, the section *justification des appréciations*, which presents the *points clés de l'audit*), material uncertainty related to going concern (ISA 570).

## Key points
- An emphasis of matter does not modify the opinion.
- "Pervasive" is the key word that separates a qualified opinion from an adverse opinion or a disclaimer.
- Review ≠ audit: a review gives limited, not reasonable, assurance.
