# Key financial ratios and performance indicators in English

**References:** IAS 33 §10 and §19-20 (earnings per share) ; IAS 7 (cash flows) ; ESMA Guidelines on APMs (ESMA/2015/1415) — règlement UE 2023/1803 for IFRS

- **Profitability**: gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue ; operating margin = operating profit (EBIT) ÷ revenue ; EBITDA margin ; **ROCE** (return on capital employed) = operating profit ÷ capital employed ; ROE (return on equity) = net profit ÷ equity.
- **Capital employed**: usually total equity + non-current liabilities (equivalently total assets − current liabilities), or equity + net debt; often averaged between opening and closing.
- **Per share data**: **basic EPS** (*résultat par action*) = profit attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent ÷ **weighted average** number of ordinary shares outstanding (IAS 33 §10, §19-20; treasury shares are excluded); diluted EPS adds potential shares (options, convertibles). P/E ratio = share price ÷ EPS ; dividend per share (DPS), dividend cover = EPS ÷ DPS, payout ratio = DPS ÷ EPS.
- **Financial structure**: net debt = borrowings (+ lease liabilities, according to the company's definition) − cash and cash equivalents ; gearing = net debt ÷ equity ; leverage = net debt ÷ EBITDA (often used in loan **covenants**) ; interest cover = operating profit ÷ net finance costs.
- **Liquidity and working capital**: current ratio, quick ratio, DSO (debtor days), DPO (creditor days), DIO (inventory days), cash conversion cycle = DIO + DSO − DPO.
- **Cash**: **free cash flow** (FCF) has no single definition; a common one is net cash from operating activities − capital expenditure (capex) + disposal proceeds, sometimes also less lease repayments and interest. Cash conversion = FCF (or operating cash flow) ÷ profit or EBITDA.

**Key formulas:** EPS = attributable profit ÷ weighted average shares ; weighted shares = opening shares + new shares × months outstanding ÷ 12 − shares bought back × months since buyback ÷ 12

## Key points
- EPS uses the weighted average, not the year-end number of shares, and profit attributable to the parent (after non-controlling interests).
- Always apply the company's own definition of an APM (FCF, net debt, adjusted EBIT).
- Gearing is sometimes defined as net debt ÷ (net debt + equity): check the definition before comparing.
